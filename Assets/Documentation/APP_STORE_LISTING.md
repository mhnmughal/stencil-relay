## 5. Description (limit 4000)

The first three lines are what shows before the More link, so they carry the pitch.

```
Stencil Relay is a quiet puzzle about light passing through layers.

You work in an optical drafting studio. Each pattern is a small stack of translucent
stencil plates on a lit table, with a projector lamp above them. Your job is to align
the plates so the lamp casts one exact geometric figure onto the board below. Every
required target has to be lit. Every forbidden sensor has to stay dark.

You have three tools. Slide a plate between its marked positions. Rotate it in quarter
turns with the brass dial. Flip it to reveal the different shape cut into its back
face. When the arrangement looks right, press Project. The room dims, the lamp comes
on, and the light composites through every layer at once so you can read the result.

Nothing here is random and nothing is hidden. The light follows a strict grid. If a
solution exists you can reason your way to it, and if you miss, the result screen
tells you which target or sensor failed and puts you straight back to the board with
your work untouched. There is no timer and no way to lose.

Thirteen stages, built one idea at a time:

A short Calibration walks you through the controls.

The first set is pure alignment. Line up one opening, then two, then learn that the
light has to pass through every layer at once.

The middle set adds blockers that carve the beam, forbidden sensors you have to route
around, and plates whose back face holds the only shape that solves the puzzle.

The last set opens up depth that shifts the opening, filter categories that sort the
light by shape, and a final pair of projectors that read the same stack of plates at
the same time.

Solve a level any way you can and it counts. Match the suggested move count and you
earn Precise. Find the exact configuration the studio intended and you earn Perfect
Register. A looser replay never overwrites a better result, and every level you finish
stays open in the Pattern Rack to revisit.

Made to be calm. A fixed camera, restrained motion, a soft original sound for each
action, and art drawn to look like frosted acrylic on a drafting table.

Completely offline. No account, no sign in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no
network of any kind. No in app purchases. Landscape only.
```

Count: about 1980.

## 7. Categories (App Store Connect)

App Store Connect lets you choose a primary category and an optional secondary
category. For games each choice carries a game subcategory.

| Slot | Category | Subcategory | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Games | **Puzzle** | The core activity is spatial logic with one correct solved state per level. This is where the audience looks. |
| **Secondary** | Games | **Board** | The play surface is a fixed grid and the pieces are placed and rotated on it, which matches how players browse Board. |

Acceptable alternatives for the secondary slot, in order of fit:
1. **Strategy** — the ratings reward planning the order of moves.
2. **Family** — clean 4+ content, no text-heavy reading, good for shared play.

Do not pick a non game category. This is a game and Apple will move it.

Google Play equivalent: Category **Puzzle**, tags/keywords may include "brain",
"logic", "board".
